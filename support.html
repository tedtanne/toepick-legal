Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. ToePick doesn't send it anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Open the Profile tab, then Account → Export My Data, and save or share the file.

How do I delete my account?

Open the Profile tab, then Account → Delete Account. This removes every skater profile, saved score and setting from your iPhone, and can't be undone.

Is the score ToePick shows my official score?

No. ToePick works out the technical score from the published rules with every grade of execution at zero, and checks a program against the published requirements. Any grade of execution or component score you see is your own estimate. Official results come only from the event's officials.
